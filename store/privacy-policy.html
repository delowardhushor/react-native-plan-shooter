Privacy Policy

PlaneShooter · Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlaneShooter (the “Game”), published by BitByteTect (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you play the Game on your mobile device. By using the Game you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store personal information about players.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (for example levels reached, scores, coins, upgrades, and settings) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device through the Game and is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the Game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads that you can watch in exchange for in-game bonuses. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service. AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain information automatically, such as:

  • Advertising ID (e.g. Google Advertising ID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and OS information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (ads viewed, clicks, crashes, performance)

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, to personalize ads where permitted, to prevent fraud, and to keep the service secure. We do not receive or control this data. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices

  • Android: open Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • iOS: open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to control app tracking requests.
  • Where required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), you may be shown a consent prompt before personalized ads are served, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can delete locally stored game data at any time by clearing the Game’s storage or uninstalling it.

5. Internet access

The Game requests internet permission only so that ads can be loaded and displayed. The Game itself can be played offline.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will work with our ad provider to address it.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. The only third party that receives data through the Game is the ad provider described above. Because progress data is stored only on your device, its security depends on the security of your device.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold personal data about you, most such requests are handled through your device settings or directly with Google as described above. You may also contact us with any questions.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
